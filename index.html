How it works

  1. Tap a square, then a letter. Any square, any letter.
  2. Every slot shows a live count of the words that still fit. Crossing slots narrow each other.
  3. When a count reaches 1, the word flips into place on its own. One lock can set off a chain across the grid.
  4. A letter that leaves a slot with zero words is dead: it bounces back. Fewer placements is a better result.

Seven ways to play

Journey

300 levels in 10 chapters. Grids grow, the letters you may use shrink, move caps tighten, and in the last chapters the counts go quiet.

Daily

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak and share your result as a row of locks, with no spoilers.

Blitz

60 seconds, one slot at a time. Lock words fast, build combos, climb from four-letter words to seven.

Zen

Endless puzzles with no lives and no timer. Pick small, medium or large and settle in.

Duel

The same puzzle on two phones. Fewer moves wins.

Standoff

One shared board, alternating turns, 30 seconds a move. Locks score, dead letters cost.

Pass & Play

Standoff on a single phone, handed back and forth. No connection needed.

Fair play

  • Lives exist only in Journey: five a day, reset at midnight.
  • Out of lives? Wait, or choose to watch a short rewarded video for two more, at most three times a day.
  • Hints in Journey come from the same rewarded videos, are free on the first three levels, and are free in Zen, three per puzzle.
  • That is the only advertising in Wordict. No banners, no pop-ups, no ads between puzzles. Every ad is one you tapped to watch.
  • No in-app purchases. Nothing is sold and nothing is paywalled.

The dictionary is 22,646 common English words of three to eight letters, built from the public-domain ENABLE list and filtered to everyday vocabulary.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address. Your progress and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Anonymous online play. Duel and Standoff use a random player id and a display name made from a fixed word list, such as "Brisk Fjord". There is no chat and no free text anywhere in the game. Room data expires two hours after a room is created.
  • Ads only when you ask. Rewarded videos are served by Google AdMob and Wordict requests non-personalised ads. Google receives your device's advertising ID when an ad is shown.
  • Not directed at children under 13.
