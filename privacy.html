Wordict Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy covers the Wordict app for Android ("Wordict", "the app"), published on Google Play by Vriea ("we"). Questions go to shumonaihaider@gmail.com.

The short version

Wordict has no account and asks for nothing about you. Your progress stays on your phone. Two parts of the app talk to the internet: rewarded video ads, which are served by Google AdMob only when you tap to watch one, and online multiplayer, which stores a small, anonymous game record on Google Firebase for a couple of hours. Everything else works offline. We do not sell data and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

What stays on your phone

Wordict stores the following in its private app storage: your settings (theme, sound, haptics, motion), Journey progress and stars, Daily results and streak, Blitz and Zen records, statistics such as words locked, your remaining lives for the day, puzzles in progress, and your online identity: a random player id and a display name the app generates for you. None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app; progress can also be cleared from Settings inside the app.

Wordict sets allowBackup to off, so Android does not include this data in cloud backup or device-to-device transfer.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Wordict shows one kind of advertisement: a rewarded video that you start yourself, by tapping Hint in Journey or by choosing "Watch an ad for 2 lives" when you have run out of lives. There are no banners, pop-ups or ads between puzzles. If you never tap those buttons, no ad is shown.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. When the ads library starts and when an ad is requested or shown, Google receives information from your device: your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and the app's package name, your IP address, and how you interact with the ad (whether it was shown, watched to the end, tapped or closed). Google also collects diagnostics and crash information about its own ads library. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and to detect fraud, under Google's privacy policy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Wordict asks Google for non-personalised ads, which means the ad is chosen from context and coarse information rather than from an advertising profile of you. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Online multiplayer (Google Firebase)

Duel and Standoff are played against another person over the internet. Wordict uses Firebase Realtime Database, a Google service, to pass the game between the two phones. There is no account and no sign-in. Your online identity is a random id generated on your phone and a display name assembled by the app from two fixed word lists, for example "Brisk Fjord". You can reshuffle the name but cannot type your own, and there is no chat or free text anywhere in the game, so nothing you write is ever shown to another player.

While you are in an online room the database holds a game record: the four-letter room code, the mode, the puzzle, both players' random ids, generated display names and colours, ready and finished flags, a heartbeat timestamp so the game can tell when someone has left, the letters placed, words locked, move counts, scores and the result. Public rooms waiting for an opponent also appear in a lobby list with the host's generated name and colour. The lobby entry is removed when the game starts or the host leaves. Room records are temporary: two hours after a room was created it can no longer be joined or listed, and its record is deleted.

As with any internet connection, Google's servers see your IP address while you are connected. Firebase's own handling of connection data is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Pass & Play, the two-player mode on a single phone, does not use the internet at all.

What we do not collect

Wordict does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and does not read anything else on your phone. It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software of our own. We cannot see who plays Wordict or how.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the two Google services above, which process it to provide ads and the multiplayer connection. We have no access to the advertising data Google collects beyond aggregate reports.

Security

Data sent to Google AdMob and Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS and TLS). Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you clear your progress in Settings or uninstall the app. Online room records are deleted two hours after the room was created. If you want a room record removed sooner, or have any other request about your data, email shumonaihaider@gmail.com with the room code and roughly when you played; we will delete it. Full steps are on the delete your data page. Advertising data is held by Google and governed by Google's privacy policy and your Android advertising settings.

Children

Wordict is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. The game shows no personalised ads to anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has used online play, email us and we will remove the room record.

Your choices

  • Play offline: Journey, Daily, Blitz, Zen and Pass & Play never connect to the internet.
  • Never tap Hint or the life refill, and no ad is ever requested.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Reshuffle your generated display name in the multiplayer lobby, or uninstall to get a new random player id.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at https://killashumu.github.io/wordict/privacy.html with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever handles data differently, this policy is updated before that version is released.
