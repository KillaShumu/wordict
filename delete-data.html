Delete your Lexlock data

Lexlock has no account, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to close. Your data is in two places: on your phone, and, if you played online, in a temporary room record that deletes itself.

On your phone: clear progress

  1. Open Lexlock.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap "Reset progress" and confirm.

This clears your Journey progress and stars, Daily results and streak, Blitz and Zen records and statistics. The app goes back to its first-run state.

On your phone: uninstall

Press and hold the Lexlock icon and choose "Uninstall", or open Android Settings > Apps > Lexlock > Uninstall. Android removes the app together with everything it stored: progress, settings, your random player id and generated display name. Lexlock does not take part in Android cloud backup, so no copy is kept there.

Online: room records

Online Duel and Standoff store a small game record on Google Firebase: the room code, the puzzle, both players' random ids, generated names and colours, moves, scores and the result. It contains nothing that identifies you. Two hours after a room was created it can no longer be joined or listed, and its record is deleted.

To have a room record removed sooner, email shumonaihaider@gmail.com with the four-letter room code (and roughly when you played, if you remember). We delete it and confirm by reply.

Advertising data

Rewarded video ads are served by Google AdMob, and the information Google receives when an ad is shown is held by Google, not by us. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads; Google's controls are described in Google's partner sites notice.

No account to delete

Lexlock has no accounts, so there is no account deletion request to make. Uninstalling the app removes your random player id; a fresh one is generated if you install again.

Questions

Email shumonaihaider@gmail.com. The full privacy policy describes everything the app stores and sends.
